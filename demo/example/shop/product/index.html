About this chair

A stacking side chair from 2013, with a moulded shell and a tapered wooden base. The shell is pressure-formed in one piece, so the back flexes slightly under load instead of holding you rigid, and the seat is deep enough to sit in through a long dinner.

Stacks four high. Felt glides come fitted; a set for hard floors is included in the box.

Materials and care

Shell in moulded veneer, lacquered or stained; legs in solid oak. The leather colorways use a semi-aniline hide that darkens a little with use.

Wipe with a damp cloth and dry straight after. Keep out of direct sun, which lightens both the stain and the leather.
