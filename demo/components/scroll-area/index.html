Scroll Area

A scroll container with a styled scrollbar of its own.

Default

Bound the __viewport, not the wrapper: it's the scroll container. The thumb fades in on hover and darkens as you engage it. Needs initScrollAreas().

The viewport stays a real scroll container, so the wheel, keyboard, touch momentum, find-in-page and scroll anchoring all keep working.

Only the bar is ours: the platform's is hidden, and a thumb element is sized and placed over it.

Why not just style the native bar? Scrollbars are composited separately, and Chrome won't reliably repaint a styled thumb when the container's hover state flips, so reveal-on-hover fails intermittently and lingers after the pointer leaves.

Dragging maps pointer movement to scroll position by the track-to-content ratio, so the thumb tracks the cursor exactly rather than drifting away from it.

A click on the track pages toward the click, the way a native bar does.

Content that fits

No overflow, no bar: the script sets data-scrollable="false". A ResizeObserver brings it back when the content grows.

Short enough to fit. No bar, and no gap where one would have been.

A list of controls

The viewport clips at its padding edge, so leave 6 for focus rings, and set animation: none on :focus-visible inside, since the ring's converge starts 9 out and gets shaved.

Component

Height goes on the viewport via viewportClassName. A ResizeObserver re-measures content that changes height on its own.

Component

data-scroll-area wires it. The bar is absolutely positioned, so revealing it never reflows the content. No tabindex needed: focusing a control inside scrolls it into view. 

<div class="scroll-area" data-scroll-area>
  <div class="scroll-area__viewport">…content…</div>
  <div class="scroll-area__bar" aria-hidden="true">
    <div class="scroll-area__thumb"></div>
  </div>
</div>
