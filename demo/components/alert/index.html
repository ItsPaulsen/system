Alert

An inline status message.

Variants

One per status (info, success, warning, error), each on its soft surface.

Your changes are saved automatically.

Your changes have been saved.

Your session expires in 5 minutes.

Payment failed. Try another card.

With title

Add a title when the message needs a heading.

Scheduled maintenance

The dashboard will be read-only on Sunday, 02:00 to 04:00.

Component

The variant picks the surface, icon, and live-region role (alert for errors, status otherwise).

Component

<div class="alert alert--info" role="status">
  <svg class="alert__icon" …>…</svg>
  <div class="alert__body">
    <p class="alert__text">Message goes here.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Variants: --info --success --warning --error -->
<!-- Errors use role="alert"; the rest use role="status" -->
